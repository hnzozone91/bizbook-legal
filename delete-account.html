Delete your BizBook account & data

BizBook — Invoice, POS & Inventory · published by Bery Health and Supplies LLC, trading as FieldHealth Africa

You can delete your BizBook account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways:

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

Settings → Delete Account

This permanently removes all business data stored on your device and signs you out. Cloud data (if you used BizBook Cloud) is removed as part of the deletion. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request by email (if you no longer have the app)

Send a deletion request from the email address linked to your BizBook Cloud account:

Request account deletion by email

Or write to hello@fieldhealthafrica.org with the subject “BizBook account deletion request”, stating the email address of the account. We may reply to confirm the request came from the account owner before deleting.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in credentials).
  • All cloud-synced business data: products, inventory, sales, invoices, clients, suppliers, expenses, payments, staff records.
  • Your AI credit balance and usage records.

What is not held by us

  • Data stored only on your device is under your control — uninstalling the app or using in-app deletion removes it.
  • Purchase and subscription records are held by Google Play, not by us — manage them in your Google account.

Timing

In-app deletion takes effect immediately on your device; the linked cloud data is removed with it. Email requests are processed within 30 days of verification. Deletion is permanent — we keep no backups of deleted accounts beyond standard short-lived infrastructure backups, which expire automatically.

Questions

Contact hello@fieldhealthafrica.org. See also the BizBook Privacy Policy.
