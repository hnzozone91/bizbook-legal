2. The short version

  • BizBook is offline-first. Your business data lives on your device by default.
  • We do not sell your data, and the app contains no advertising.
  • Cloud features (backup, sync, multi-device, AI) are optional and only run when you create a BizBook account or use an AI feature.
  • Crash reporting and usage analytics are both off until you say yes, and both can be switched off again at any time.
  • You can export or permanently delete all your data from inside the app at any time.

3. Data the app handles

a) Business data you enter. Products, inventory, batches, sales, invoices, estimates, clients, suppliers, expenses, payments, bank and cash balances, and staff records you choose to add. This is your operational data.

b) Account data (only if you create an account). Your email address, and either an encrypted password or a Google sign-in, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) to sign you in and sync your data. We never store your password in readable form.

  • If you sign in with a password, account emails (sign-up verification codes and password-reset codes) are delivered through our email provider (Brevo), which processes your email address solely to deliver those messages.
  • If you use "Continue with Google", Google confirms your identity to us and returns your email address and basic profile details. We ask Google for nothing beyond that: no contacts, no calendar, no Drive, no other account data. Google's own privacy policy governs what Google does with the sign-in. You can use a password instead, and you can set a password afterwards so the account is never locked to Google.

c) Your email address for the user manual (optional). The last step of setup asks, separately and optionally, for an address to send you the BizBook user manual. This is not the business email printed on your invoices; leaving it blank skips it entirely, and there is no pre-ticked box anywhere in that flow. If you provide it, we store it on your device and send it to our Supabase project together with the language you chose, your business type, the app version, and a short installation tag used to limit abuse of the form. No business data of any kind is sent with it: no sales, no products, no customers, no figures. We use the address only to send you the manual and occasional important notices about the app, and we never share, sell, or rent it. To be removed, email us at the address in section 11.

d) Voice input (only if you use voice entry). If you use the optional voice-entry feature to dictate a sale or expense, your speech is converted to text using your device's speech-recognition service (which may be provided by Google on Android). We receive only the resulting text, which you review before anything is saved. The microphone is used only while you actively hold the record button.

e) Images you submit to AI features (only if you use them). When you use Scan-to- Inventory or Invoice/Receipt OCR, the photo you take is sent, through our secure backend proxy, to our AI provider (Anthropic) to extract text and product details. Natural- language report questions are sent the same way. Images and queries are used only to return your result; they are not used to train AI models, and they are not kept once the request has been answered.

f) Crash reports (optional, off until you agree). If you accept the first-launch prompt, a crash sends the error, its stack trace, your app version, Android version, device model, and locale to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service. It never sends a user or business identifier, so a report cannot be tied back to you or your shop, and it never sends screenshots, product names, prices, totals, customer details, or anything from a sale, invoice, or payment. Collection is switched off in the app's manifest and enabled at runtime only after you agree. Turning it off in Settings → Privacy stops it immediately and deletes anything still queued.

g) Usage analytics (optional, off by default). If you turn it on, we record the names of a few setup milestones — opening the app, finishing setup, making a first sale — with the app version and how long the step took. Nothing else. No amounts, no product or customer names, no free text; the table that stores it has no column that could hold them. It goes to our own Supabase project, not to an advertising network.

We do not collect contacts, precise location, health or clinical records, or browsing history. BizBook is a business-operations tool, not a health-records system.

Device permissions the app may request, and why:

  • Camera — scanning product barcodes and QR attendance codes (processed on-device, nothing uploaded), and photographing products or supplier invoices for the optional AI features (uploaded only when you confirm).
  • Microphone — optional voice entry only (see d). Never recorded in the background.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices — connecting to a Bluetooth receipt printer you choose. Receipt data goes only to that printer; nothing else is discovered or contacted.
  • Notifications — local reminders you configure (low stock, expiring batches, invoice due dates). Generated on-device.

Every permission is optional: denying it disables only the matching feature.

5. Where your data is stored, and how it is protected

  • On your device — always. The database is held in BizBook's private application storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps. It is not encrypted at rest, so somebody holding the unlocked handset could in principle read the file. Set a device screen lock, and use BizBook's own PIN or biometric lock (Settings → Security) to add a second barrier. We would rather say this plainly than imply a protection the app does not yet provide.
  • In the cloud (Supabase) — only when you sign in and sync. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and access is protected by per-account row-level security, so one account can never read another's records.
  • AI provider (Anthropic) — receives only the specific image or question for a single request, through our proxy, and does not retain it for training.

6. Payments

BizBook never sees your card number or your mobile-money PIN. We do not build or operate a payment system; licensed processors handle the money.

  • Mobile money (Cameroon and the wider CEMAC region). Subscriptions and credit packs are bought on our website at fieldhealthafrica.org/bizbook, where CamPay processes MTN and Orange Money payments. CamPay receives the mobile-money number and confirms the payment to us; we record only that a payment succeeded and what it entitles you to.
  • Google Play Billing. Where in-app purchasing is enabled, Google handles the payment, the renewal, and any refund, and tells us only that a purchase was made. Google's privacy policy governs your payment details.

Whichever route applies, the app stores only your plan and your remaining AI credits.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the features you use:

  • Supabase — cloud authentication, database, and storage (only if you create an account), and the store for the manual-email list and opt-in analytics.
  • Google — sign-in, if you choose "Continue with Google" (b); crash reporting through Firebase Crashlytics, if you agree to it (f); and payment processing where Play Billing is used (section 6).
  • Brevo — delivery of account emails and the user manual to your email address.
  • Anthropic — AI text and vision processing, only when you use an AI feature.
  • CamPay — mobile-money payment processing for purchases made on our website.
  • Your device's speech-recognition service — voice-to-text conversion, only when you use voice entry.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for advertising.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Stay local: use BizBook entirely offline without an account. Your data never leaves your device.
  • Export and back up: Settings → Export Data (CSV), and Settings → Backup for a complete one-tap backup (a ZIP of every table) you can keep anywhere. Free on every plan, because your records should never be trapped inside our app.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account permanently removes all local data and signs you out; cloud data, if any, is removed on the next sync. This cannot be undone. If you no longer have the app, request deletion at hnzozone91.github.io/bizbook-legal/delete-account.html.
  • Withdraw consent: Settings → Privacy turns crash reporting and analytics off again at any time, effective immediately.
  • Skip AI: every AI feature is optional and has a manual alternative.
  • Leave the manual mailing list: email us and we will delete your address.

9. Data retention

Local data is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is kept while your account is active and removed when you delete your account. Manual-email addresses are kept until you ask us to remove them.

10. Children

BizBook is intended for business owners and staff. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
