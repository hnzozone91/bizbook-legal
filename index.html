BizBook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

BizBook (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a business-management app — invoicing, point of sale, and inventory — published by Bery Health and Supplies LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company trading as FieldHealth Africa, of 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and your choices. Plain language, no surprises.

1. The short version

  • BizBook is offline-first. Your business data lives on your device by default.
  • We do not sell your data, and the app contains no advertising.
  • Cloud features (backup, sync, AI) are optional and only run when you create a BizBook Cloud account or use an AI feature.
  • You can export or permanently delete all your data from inside the app at any time.

2. Data the app handles

a) Business data you enter. Products, inventory, batches, sales, invoices, estimates, clients, suppliers, expenses, payments, bank/cash balances, and staff records you choose to add.

b) Account data (only if you enable Cloud). Your email address and an encrypted password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) to sign you in and sync your data. We never store your password in readable form. Account emails — sign-up verification codes and password-reset codes — are delivered through our email service provider (Brevo), which processes your email address solely to deliver those messages.

b2) Voice input (only if you use voice entry). If you use the optional voice-entry feature to dictate a sale or expense, your speech is converted to text using your device's speech-recognition service (which may be provided by Google on Android). We receive only the resulting text, which you review before anything is saved. The microphone is used only while you actively hold the record button.

c) Images you submit to AI features (only if you use them). When you use Scan-to-Inventory or Invoice/Receipt OCR, the photo is sent — through our secure backend proxy — to our AI provider (Anthropic) to extract text and product details. Natural-language report questions are sent the same way. Images and queries are used only to return your result; they are not used to train AI models.

d) Technical/diagnostic data. Basic crash and error information to keep the app stable. No advertising identifiers are collected.

We do not collect contacts, precise location, health/clinical records, or browsing history. BizBook is a business-operations tool, not a health-records system.

Device permissions the app may request, and why:

  • Camera — scanning product barcodes / QR attendance codes (processed on-device, nothing uploaded) and photographing products or supplier invoices for the optional AI features (uploaded only when you confirm).
  • Microphone — optional voice entry only (see b2). Never recorded in the background.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices — connecting to a Bluetooth receipt printer you choose. Receipt data goes only to that printer; nothing else is discovered or contacted.
  • Notifications — local reminders you configure (low stock, expiring batches, invoice due dates). Generated on-device.

Every permission is optional: denying it disables only the matching feature.

4. Where your data is stored

  • On your device (encrypted local database) — always.
  • In the cloud (Supabase) — only when you sign in to BizBook Cloud and sync. Encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS); protected by per-account access controls.
  • AI provider (Anthropic) — receives only the specific image or question for a single request, via our proxy; not retained for training.

5. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the features you use: Supabase (cloud auth/database/storage, only if you enable Cloud), Brevo (delivery of account emails — verification and password-reset codes — only if you enable Cloud), Anthropic (AI processing, only when you use an AI feature), Google Play (payment processing), and your device's speech-recognition service (voice-to-text, only when you use voice entry). We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Stay local: use BizBook entirely offline without a Cloud account — your data never leaves your device.
  • Export: Settings → Export Data (CSV), and Settings → Backup for a complete one-tap backup (ZIP of every table) you can keep anywhere — free on every plan.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account permanently removes all local data and signs you out; cloud data (if any) is removed on the next sync. This cannot be undone. No longer have the app? Request account & data deletion here.
  • Skip AI: every AI feature is optional and has a manual alternative.

7. Data retention

Local data is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is kept while your account is active and removed when you delete your account.

8. Children

BizBook is intended for business owners and staff. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, noted in the app.
